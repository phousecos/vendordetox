How it works

The engagement runs on data you already have

The lift on your team is light — finance can pull most of it in an afternoon.

1

You provide three things

12 months of vendor payments from AP, an export from your identity system (Okta, Microsoft, or Google Workspace), and your renewal calendar.

2

We reconcile pay against use

Payment records show what you're buying. Your identity system shows what people actually sign into. The gap between them is where the money is.

3

We surface the leverage

Redundant tools, subscriptions on autopilot, premium tiers nobody uses, and renewals where the contract has quietly weakened your position.

4

You get a plan

A prioritized written report and a live debrief — what to cut now, and what to renegotiate at renewal. You own the findings entirely.
